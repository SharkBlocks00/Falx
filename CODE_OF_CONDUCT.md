# Falx Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of Falx, pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community - one where a first-time contributor filing their first issue feels just as respected as a long-time maintainer reviewing a pull request.

## Our Values

Falx is a small project, but the way people treat each other in it matters regardless of size. These are the principles we try to hold ourselves to:

- **Be welcoming.** We strive to be a community that welcomes and supports people of all backgrounds and identities. This includes, but is not limited to, members of any race, ethnicity, culture, national origin, color, immigration status, sex, age, size, family status, political belief, religion, and mental or physical ability.
- **Be respectful.** Disagreement is not an excuse for poor behavior or personal attacks. A community where people feel uncomfortable or threatened is not a productive one. Respect that people have differences of opinion, and that every design or implementation choice carries trade-offs and costs - there is rarely a single right answer.
- **Be direct, but professional.** We're direct about disagreements over code, ideas, and design. Directness is valuable when it improves the project, but it can be used to intimidate, belittle, or condescend. Aim to be honest about a problem while remaining professional about the person raising or facing it.
- **Be careful in the words that you choose.** We are a community of professionals, and we conduct ourselves professionally. Be kind to others. Do not insult or put down other participants. Harassment and other exclusionary behavior are not acceptable - see below for specifics.
- **When we disagree, try to understand why.** Disagreements happen, both social and technical. It is important that we resolve disagreements constructively. Remember that we're different; the strength of a small open-source project comes from its varied community, of people from a wide range of backgrounds. Different people have different perspectives on issues. Being unable to understand why someone holds a viewpoint doesn't mean that they're wrong. Try to understand why people think the way they do, and work from there toward a solution everyone can live with.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences, especially in issue discussions and code review.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback - critique the code and the idea, not the person.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall Falx community.
- Assuming good faith: most disagreements are the result of a misunderstanding, not malice.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission ("doxxing").
- Dismissing or attacking a person on the basis of their identity, or discouraging any individual from participating in the project on the basis of who they are.
- Repeated, deliberate off-topic behavior after being asked to stop.
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behavior.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

This is not an exhaustive list. If something feels like a violation of the spirit of this document even if it isn't listed explicitly, it probably is.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces - including issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review comments on this repository - and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces, such as using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns about conduct in this project, please report it as soon as possible:

1. **Preferred:** open a [private GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/SharkBlocks00/Falx/security/advisories/new) for this repository. Despite the name, this is a confidential channel visible only to the maintainer, and is the most reliable way to reach them directly through GitHub.
2. **Alternative:** contact the maintainer directly via their GitHub profile.

Please do not report Code of Conduct concerns in public issues or pull requests.

When reporting, please include, if possible:

- Your contact information (if you're comfortable sharing it).
- The names (GitHub usernames) of any individuals involved, and witnesses if applicable.
- A description of the incident, including links to the relevant issue, PR, or comment where it occurred.
- Any additional context you believe is relevant.

All reports will be handled with discretion. You should expect an acknowledgment within a reasonable timeframe. Given the size of this project, there is currently a single maintainer responsible for enforcement rather than a dedicated committee - this will be revisited if the project and its community grow.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The maintainer will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action judged to be in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project (issues, pull requests, discussions) for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project.

## Appeals

If you believe you have been unfairly accused of violating this Code of Conduct, you may appeal the decision by contacting the maintainer through the same reporting channel above with a concise description of your grievance. Appeals will be handled with the same discretion as original reports.
